Using veterinary medicines safely

Dose depends on the species, the body weight and the condition being treated. Never give an animal medicine meant for a different species or for people.

After treatment, milk, meat and eggs must not enter the food chain until the withdrawal period on the label has passed.

Keep medicines in their original packaging, away from children, pets and direct sunlight. Check the label for storage temperature and expiry date.
