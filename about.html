About Kiwenda Vet Shop

We are a team of veterinarians and pharmacists who deliver medicines for animals.

Our story

Kiwenda Vet Shop started on a small scale with one question: why were so many farmers struggling to find effective treatments for their animals?

Today we distribute tablets, liquids, injectables and vaccines for the animals that share our homes and feed our communities. Every product is distributed with veterinarians and pharmacies to ensure that every animal gets the right medicine at the right dose.

We sell through licensed veterinarians, so every animal gets the right medicine at the right dose.

What guides our work

Our mission

To distribute safe, effective medicines that protect animal health and the people who depend on animals.

Our vision

A world where every pet, herd and flock has access to quality veterinary care.

Our values

Safety first, honest labelling, respect for animals and responsible use of antibiotics.
