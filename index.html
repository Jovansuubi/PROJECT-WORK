Medicines that keep animals healthy and farms productive.

Kiwenda Vet Shop distributes veterinary drugs for pets, livestock and working animals. We supply them directly to the farmers around Kiwenda and the neigbhouring villages.

See what we make Contact us

Who we supply medicines for

Dogs and cats

Treatments for infections, parasites, pain and skin problems.

Cattle, sheep and goats

Injectables and oral drugs for herd and flock health.

Poultry

Vitamins and water-soluble medicines for healthy flocks.

Horses

Dewormers and pain relief for sport and working horses.

Why farmers choose Kiwenda Vet Shop

Every batch is tested

Each batch is checked for strength and purity before it leaves our shop.

Clear dosing by weight

Labels show the dose for each body weight, so there is less room for error.

Vets on call

Our veterinary team answers questions from farms during working hours.

Not sure which product fits your animal?

Send us a message and our veterinary team will point you in the right direction.
